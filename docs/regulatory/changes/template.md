# CR-NNNN: Title in a few words

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | proposed |
| Requested | YYYY-MM-DD, by role |
| Source | problem report, CAPA, requirement change or improvement |
| Decision | pending |
| Commits | none yet |

## Description

What changes, in a few sentences a reviewer outside the team understands.

## Reason

Why the change is needed, and what happens if it is not made.

## Impact analysis

### Requirements

The SRS requirements added, changed or withdrawn, by ID, and the ones whose verification must be
repeated.

### Software items and safety class

The items changed, and whether any item's class or segregation changes.

### Risk

The hazards and risk controls affected, by ID; new causes of hazardous situations; whether an
estimate in the risk file changes.

### SOUP

SOUP items added, removed or upgraded, with their known-anomaly review.

### Interfaces and data formats

Changes to the control protocol, the configuration schema, the readout format or the list-mode
format, and their compatibility.

### Documents

The documents that change, and their new versions.

### Regulatory

Whether the change alters the intended use or a safety-relevant function, and whether it may be a
significant change for the notified body or need a new submission to the FDA.

## Verification

The tests, checks and measurements that show the change works and broke nothing.

## Decision

Who decided, when, and on what grounds.
