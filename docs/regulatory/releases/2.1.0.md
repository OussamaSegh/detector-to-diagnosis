# MiniDAQ 2.1.0 release record

The record of SDP section 8 for release 2.1.0, the first minor release since 2.0: the fourth
exercise of every chapter from 26 to 40, the book's measurement folders, the tighter page
layout, the C++ projector's output without a copy (CR-0034), the release soak of 100 minutes
(CR-0035, which closes MDQ-PR-064), and the fixes of the review of 2.0.1. Evidence paths
are relative to this directory's parent, `docs/regulatory/`; the files are in `measurements/release-2.1.0/` and were
written by `tools/release_checks.sh` unless the notes below say otherwise. Checked with
`tools/release_record.py check` (Exercise 14.5's `check()`) before the tag was made.

## Release

MiniDAQ 2.1.0, commit dae4498b4079ae1d7a49a825ce6bf75cdb0052ac, tag book-v2.1.0

The release checks ran on this commit with a clean working tree (`commit.txt`); the parts that
passed: 1, 2, 3, 4 (`release-checks.txt`), in one worktree (`commit.txt`, `commit-part-2.txt`
to `commit-part-4.txt`). Every part passed at its first verdict. Parts 3 and 4 each started
twice: the first run of each was stopped by a session's two-hour limit before its verdict, and
`resume` ran it again; a run stopped without a verdict is not a failure under SDP section 8
(evidence README). An earlier start on fbe436b was stopped by hand in part 1, before any
verdict, when CI on that commit failed (MDQ-PR-086); the checks were started again on its fix.

The changes since 2.0.1 (tag `book-v2.0.1`):

- **CR-0034** (SRS-REC-009, SRS version 38): the C++ projector's `forward` and `back` take an
  optional `out`, written in place and never converted; a converted, read-only, wrongly shaped
  or overlapping output is refused (RC-43). It changes the reconstruction tool (TL-31), not a
  shipped software item.
- **CR-0035** (SRS-SYS-003, SRS-PERF-022): the release soak lasts 100 minutes, and ACC-04's
  share of 99% is judged over 600 intervals. It closes **MDQ-PR-064**.
- **MDQ-PR-081 to MDQ-PR-083** (the review of 2.0.1): the record skeleton's Build section, the
  resume of a failed whole run, the codebase survey across renames.
- **MDQ-PR-084** (relevant to safety, RC-47): four validations matched with `$` let a value
  with a final newline pass; they use `fullmatch`, and the coding standard gains rule 3.6.
- **MDQ-PR-085** (the internal audit's N-1, CAPA-0006): CI on main was red for six days
  without a record noticing it; the eight OpenSSL findings have triage decisions, and SDP
  version 22 says when the unit suite runs and the CI result is read.
- **MDQ-PR-086**: Exercise 29.4's test of the GIL made one observation of a scheduler.
- The book: the fourth exercises of Chapters 26 to 40, Chapter 28's "How many subsets",
  Chapter 29's "The crossing, measured", the measurement folders of Chapters 1, 6 and 22
  (erratum E-010), and margins of 0.7 in (794 pages).

No source of a shipped software item changed since 2.0.1 (`coverage-review.md`). The commits on
top of dae4498 up to the tag change no product code: the evidence, the closure of CAPA-0005,
MDQ-PR-087 (a label of the record's skeleton and its test), the audit's version 2, this record
and the version strings.

## Verification

Notes on the results:

- Builds and tests: the six presets built and passed their tests (`run_all.txt`), the product
  preset too (`product.txt`); clang-tidy 18 and cppcheck 2.13 found nothing
  (`static-analysis.txt`); every exercise starter fails at least one test and none hangs
  (`starters.txt`).
- Stress and properties: 100 repetitions under TSan, 101 tests, all passed; the wide property
  search, 12 tests, passed.
- Structural coverage: 90.9% of lines and 81.2% of branches of the shipped code
  (`coverage.txt`). No class B line changed since 2.0.1, so the review (`coverage-review.md`)
  has no line to examine; four items differ from 2.0.1 by one branch each, three lower and one
  higher, with no change to their source.
- Performance: the regression gate against `book-v2.0.1`, interleaved, passed
  (`perf-gate.txt`): six benchmarks, speeds 0.981 to 1.063 of 2.0.1, none significant. The
  pipeline ran at a median of 42.9 M singles/s (SRS-PERF-020), and the writer at 19.07 M
  coincidences/s at its slowest (SRS-PERF-021).
- Soak: 100 minutes at 1 M singles/s, no loss, no fault, memory flat (30.5 to 31.3 MiB, trend
  0.000 MiB/h); the 99th percentile at most 10 ms in 597 of 600 intervals (99.5%, 99% required;
  ACC-04, SRS-PERF-022), the highest 14.22 ms. In the three intervals above the limit, the
  largest one-second run-queue wait of one thread was 22.6 to 26.3 ms, the steal under 1.2%: the
  rare peaks of MDQ-PR-064, which CR-0035's criterion allows for.
- System tests: 38 test cases, all passed. End to end: 33 of 33 checks of both acquisitions
  passed, the records equal to the reference; the image's radius 80.25 mm, center over
  periphery 1.0419.
- Fuzzing: nine targets, 600 s each, no crash; `fuzz_documents` ran 1,256,374 inputs
  (CAPA-0005).
- Acceptance: 9 of 9 (`acceptance.md`). Reproducible build: two builds of the product preset
  in two checkouts, identical archives (`repro/repro.txt`).
- CI on GitHub: ci, gates, codeql, pages and kdp green on dae4498 (`ci-run.txt`); the release
  commit's own runs are checked before the tag.

## Risk

All 17 hazards of the risk file are closed (`risk/hazards.json`, test `docs.risk`). One risk
control changed: RC-43 (H-13) now covers the projector's output, refused when converted,
read-only, wrongly shaped or overlapping its input (CR-0034), verified by `tools/test_recon_cpp.py`.
No hazard was added. The threat model (version 18) was reviewed for this release: no change adds
an element, flow or asset of the product (CR-0034 is in the reconstruction tool, CR-0035 in the
release checks), so it stands. The overall residual risk is acceptable, as judged for 2.0.0 and
unchanged by this release.

## Build

The release is built from the tag with the presets, as the checks did:

```sh
git checkout book-v2.1.0
ci/run_all.sh                                     # the six presets, tests and documentation tests
ci/run_all.sh product                             # the product build, without the simulator
tools/static_analysis.sh build/clang-release
cmake --preset product && cmake --build --preset product     # the shipped binaries (CR-0029)
tools/repro_check.sh OUT                                     # two builds, compared byte for byte
tools/make_sbom.py --validate -o minidaq-2.1.0.cdx.json
tools/release_checks.sh OUT 600 resume                        # this record's evidence
tools/acceptance.py OUT                                       # the acceptance verdict
book/build.sh all both && book/build.sh boox both             # the book and the solutions
```
