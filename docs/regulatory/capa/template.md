# CAPA-NNNN: Title in a few words

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Opened | YYYY-MM-DD, by role |
| Source | problem reports or audit finding |
| Owner | role |
| Effectiveness check | YYYY-MM-DD or release |

## Problem

What happened, where, and how it was found.

## Investigation

Every occurrence found, how the search was done, and the chain of causes.

## Root cause

The cause in the process that the actions remove. Not "human error".

## Correction

What was done to the problems found.

## Corrective action

The change to a rule, a check or a procedure, with the files it changes.

## Preventive action

Where else the cause could act, and what was changed there; or "none", with the reason.

## Verification of the actions

That the actions were done, and that they weaken no requirement or risk control.

## Effectiveness check

Criterion: what will show that the actions worked.
Due: when it is checked.
Result: filled in at the check.

## Closure

Who closed the CAPA, when, and on what grounds.
