## 4. Where the extra picoseconds come from

minidaq_sim with --ctr 0: no time blur; what remains is the flight of each photon inside its crystal to the point where it interacts (the simulator adds path length to the depth of interaction)
prompts 135,510; signed time difference: SD 101.0 ps, quartiles -14, 0, 14 ps
variances add under convolution: SD sqrt(127.4^2 + 101.0^2) = 162.6 ps, which as a Gaussian is a FWHM of 382.8 ps
measured in section 3: 303.4 ps (the fit is of the core of the peak, so it weighs the depth's long tail less than the SD does)
