## 1. Normalization scan: crystal efficiencies from fan sums

6 runs: 10,484,053 prompts, 898,977 delayeds; read and summed in 3.7 s.
Fan sum (prompts minus delayeds) per crystal: mean 1,040, so a Poisson error of about 3.1%.
True efficiencies: mean 0.8994, rms spread 4.91%; relative to their ring's mean, rms 5.45%.
Found against true (each relative to its ring): rms error 3.38%, worst 14.0%, mean +0.000%.
Ring means, left to the geometric factors: 0.8896 to 0.9064.
Per bin, the mean efficiency product over its ring pairs: rms error 1.44% (the products of 48 ring pairs average the crystals' errors).
Geometric factors: 0.000 to 2.307 (slices x columns, mean 1).

## 2. Patient-like scan: randoms

Run: 5.000 s; 18,069,637 singles in 435 to 650 keV, of which 13.5% in a prompt coincidence; window 4000 ps, fixed.
Binned prompts 1,143,163: trues 751,117, scatters 274,260, randoms 117,786 (10.3% of the prompts).
Randoms estimates against the truth, in the binned field of view:

| Estimate | Randoms | Against the truth |
|---|---:|---:|
| delayed window | 126,181 | +7.1% |
| 2 tau S_a S_b / t, all singles | 154,413 | +31.1% |
| the same, singles in a prompt coincidence subtracted | 115,631 | -1.8% |

Per bin: mean 0.0284 true randoms. The delayeds scatter about the singles estimate by 0.175 rms, Poisson noise on means of 0.0279; the singles estimate has no noise of its own (its singles are thousands per crystal).
Shape over s (sum of all slices and views): singles estimate / truth from 0.884 to 1.095 across the columns, rms 4.2% (truth Poisson about 4.4% per column).
