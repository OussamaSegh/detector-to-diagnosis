## 1. Geometry: a point source at (40, -25) mm in air

| Lines at radius | x found | y found | Distance from the axis, found / true | FWHM x | FWHM y |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 150 mm | 38.58 | -24.11 | 0.9645 | 2.1 mm | 1.9 mm |
| 156 mm | 40.11 | -25.06 | 1.0028 | 2.0 mm | 1.8 mm |

With the lines at the front faces the point comes out 3.6% too close to the axis: the photons interact on average below the front face, so the lines are at about 156 mm. OSEM, 8 subsets, 10 iterations, 1 mm pixels; the FWHM is of the reconstructed point at that iteration, not the scanner's resolution (Chapter 30).

## 2. A phantom of hot rods: OSEM iteration by iteration

Lines at 156 mm. Data: slices 20 to 74 summed, 1,044,524 prompts, of which 684,140 trues; the model's additive term (randoms from the singles, scatter scaled to the tails) 366,418. Image 128 x 128 pixels of 2 mm. Rods of radius 12, 8 and 5 mm at 4 times the background; their regions are the inner half radius. Background: 1860 pixels within 60 mm of the axis and 12 mm or more from a rod's edge.
