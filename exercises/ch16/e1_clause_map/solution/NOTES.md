# Exercise 16.1: notes

**Asked for design validation.** Show the plan, not the tests: "MiniDAQ is a component of the
scanner, and validation is of the scanner in its use environment: the system tests with phantoms
and the clinical workflow. MiniDAQ's tests are verification, traced in the trace matrix. Here is
where the system validation plan names MiniDAQ's part." If the plan does not exist yet, say so and
say who owns it; an auditor accepts a gap with an owner far more readily than evidence that turns
out to be something else.

**Why the map has gaps.** Clauses such as 6.2 (competence) and 8.2.1 (feedback) are the
organization's, not the software's: a team's documents are evidence for them only as a part of a
larger system. A map that claims every clause from a repository is a map nobody checked.
